Synthetic patient data, generated and graded.

An R/Shiny app that creates realistic synthetic health data with five generative models, then measures the trade-off between how useful and how private the result is.

What it does

Generate, compare, and stress-test synthetic data.

Models

Five generators, two at a time

Bayesian network, CART, random forest, parametric, and Gaussian copula. Pick any two and see them evaluated side by side.

Fidelity

A dial you control

Four fidelity levels trade realism for privacy. A built-in sweep scores every level and plots the whole trade-off as a curve.

Evaluation

Graded against unseen data

Distribution match, association preservation, predictive utility, and privacy — all scored against a holdout the generators never saw.

Privacy

Local-first by design

The downloadable build runs on your machine with a portable R runtime. No install, no upload — your data stays put.

Under the hood

The methods, in the open.

Generative models

  • Bayesian netHill-climbing structure with a Bayesian fit (bnlearn)
  • CARTTree-based sequential synthesis (synthpop)
  • Random forestEnsemble sequential synthesis (synthpop)
  • ParametricDistribution-based synthesis (synthpop)
  • CopulaRank-based Gaussian copula

How it's graded

  • DistributionKS distance (continuous), JS divergence (categorical)
  • AssociationMixed pairwise change (Pearson, Cramér's V, eta)
  • UtilityTSTR vs TRTR retention on a real holdout
  • PrivacyGower distance-to-closest-record, exact matches
  • MembershipDistance-based membership-inference AUC
How it works

Five steps from data to a defensible answer.

Load

Your dataset, or the built-in synthetic sample.

Split

Set aside an unseen holdout as the yardstick.

Generate

Two models learn the training set and make new rows.

Set fidelity

Choose the realism / privacy balance.

Evaluate

Grade against the holdout on both axes.

Open to contributions

Built to be extended.

This is a research and teaching tool meant to grow. A few directions that would plug in cleanly:

  • Add a generator — for example a deep model (CTGAN / TVAE) as a sixth option.
  • Add metrics — new privacy attacks, or downstream utility for specific clinical endpoints.
  • Expand data handling — richer missing-data strategies, longitudinal or multi-table support.
  • Reporting — a one-click summary report for documentation or governance review.
